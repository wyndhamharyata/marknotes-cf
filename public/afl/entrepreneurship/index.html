Career Plan · The 5D Framework

One unbroken line — from dream to determination

Dream

By 2035, become a Distinguished Engineer — designing the platforms that 10M+ people quietly depend on, and reaching the top of the technical ladder without ever stepping away from the code.

Desire

I want that summit badly — to be the engineer teams escalate to when the problem is hardest, to watch systems I architected still running years later, and to turn the juniors I mentor into the next generation of leads.

Drive

So I turn that want into motion — one career-defining win every year: a system led to production, a design doc adopted org-wide, or a conference talk landed. Each one a rung from Senior → Staff → Principal.

Discipline

Those yearly wins are built from unbroken habits: ship code every week, publish one deep technical piece a month, dissect a large-scale system each quarter, and review my own metrics monthly.

Determination

And when a promo slips or a project gets killed, I re-scope the timeline — never the destination. Reassess at every level, adapt the route, and keep climbing to the summit.
