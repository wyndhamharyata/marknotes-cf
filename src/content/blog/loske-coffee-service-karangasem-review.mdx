What makes the best coffee shops? For me: *good coffee, good food, not too far, and a good vibe.*

So, in theory, Loske should be the best of all. Good coffee? Check. Easy to access food? We have **Gallus** *(Fried Chicken Chain)* under the same roof, so check, and incidentally, also very close to my place, literally under 5 minutes. So that also check.

So what gives it 3.5 stars? Well, it's just a bit disjointed.
